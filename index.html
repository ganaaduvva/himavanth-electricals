What we do

Six services our clients call us for most — alongside a full range of electrical installation, testing and maintenance work.

Cable Laying

LT and HT cable trenching, tray routing, dressing, tagging and installation to project and safety standards.

Panel Installations

LT & HT panel erection — positioning, busbar connections, termination, testing and commissioning.

Manpower Supply

Skilled electricians, technicians, welders and fitters supplied for on-site industrial and commercial work.

Thermography Testing

Infrared inspection of panels and equipment to catch overheating faults before they become failures.

Energy Audits

Assessing power usage and quality to identify losses, inefficiencies and improvement opportunities.

Safety Audits

Site electrical safety review against standards, flagging risks before they cause downtime or harm.
