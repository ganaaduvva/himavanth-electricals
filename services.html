Our Services

Full electrical installation, testing and maintenance services for industrial, commercial and residential projects.

Cable Laying

Professional laying and routing of LT and HT power cables for industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects. Our services include cable trenching, cable tray routing, dressing, tagging and proper installation as per project requirements and safety standards.

Cable Termination — HT & LT

Complete HT and LT cable termination services using suitable termination kits and accessories. We undertake cable preparation, gland installation, lug crimping, termination, insulation testing and commissioning to ensure safe and reliable power connections.

Earth Pit Installation

Installation of reliable electrical earthing systems for buildings, industries, electrical panels, transformers, DG sets and other equipment. We provide conventional and maintenance-free earthing solutions designed to achieve effective fault-current dissipation and equipment protection.

LT & HT Panel Erection

Complete erection and installation of LT and HT electrical panels, including positioning, assembly, busbar connections, cable termination, interconnections, testing and commissioning. Our team focuses on safe installation and proper electrical clearances.

UPS Installation

Installation, testing and commissioning of online UPS systems for critical electrical and industrial applications. Services include UPS positioning, battery bank installation, cabling, bypass connections, testing and system commissioning.

Transformer Erection

Professional transformer erection and installation services for distribution and industrial power systems. This includes transformer positioning, assembly, cable/busbar connections, earthing, protection connections, testing and commissioning.

Transformer Oil Filtration

Transformer oil filtration and purification services to improve insulation performance and extend transformer service life. The process helps remove moisture, suspended particles and other contaminants from transformer oil.

DG Erection

Complete DG set erection and installation services, including equipment positioning, electrical cabling, earthing, AMF/control panel connections, battery connections and testing. We support reliable standby power solutions for commercial and industrial applications.

AMC — Annual Maintenance Contract

Comprehensive electrical maintenance contracts for industries, commercial facilities and infrastructure installations. AMC services can cover electrical panels, transformers, DG sets, UPS systems, earthing systems and other electrical equipment, with preventive maintenance and troubleshooting support.

Thermography Testing Services

Infrared inspection of panels and equipment to identify overheating faults, loose connections and developing failures before they cause downtime or damage.

Power Quality Analysis & Energy Audit

Assessment of power usage and quality to identify losses, inefficiencies and improvement opportunities — helping reduce costs and improve system reliability.

Safety Audit

Site electrical safety review against applicable standards, flagging risks before they cause downtime, damage or harm to personnel.
